Questions

Good to know

What does it cost?

Nothing. It's a free game for our playgroup, run by its host. No shop, no ads, no collection to buy: every card is available.

Who can play?

Anyone with the group code from the host. You create your account with it in the game, and that's it.

Do I have to download every update?

No. The game tells you when there's a new version and updates itself in place, downloading only what changed.

Windows says the app is unknown. Is it safe?

The game isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows SmartScreen asks first. Choose More info → Run anyway.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Quick Play starts a game against bots in seconds, in Commander or any other format, and the bots draft and play Sealed with you too.

Can I bring my own decks?

Yes. Paste a list or a Moxfield or Archidekt link into the deck builder. The game checks legality and the bracket for you.

My connection dropped. Is my game lost?

No. Start the game again and the server hands your game back where it was.

I found a bug. What now?

Use Report bug in the main menu: it sends the details straight to the host.

See what's in it

Formats, house rules, seasons, stipulations and five tables to play on.
