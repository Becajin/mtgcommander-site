Features

Everything at the table

Every format, your group's own rules and seasons, a table that comes alive, and the things that just work in the background.

All formats

Commander, Constructed and Limited

Pick a format, pick a deck and a game starts. Quick Play puts you against bots in seconds; Create table lets friends join.

  • Commander for 2 to 6 players, with more than 200 official precons and decks from Game Knights and other shows
  • Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Brawl, Canadian Highlander and casual
  • Draft, Sealed, cubes and Beejlander, then Swiss rounds against the bots or each other
  • One game, best of three or best of five

Multiplayer and duels

One table for everyone

Every battlefield stays in view: life totals, commanders, the stack and whose turn it is. Two players get a duel table, five or six get side tablets that open to their full battlefield.

  • Commander damage, tax, partners, the monarch and the initiative tracked for you
  • Bots fill any seat and play their decks seriously
  • Spells and triggers trail from their source to what they aim at

Tables

Gather your group

Open a table with your format and rules. Friends join, everyone picks a deck and clicks Ready; empty seats take a bot or wait for one more player.

  • Each seat shows its player, their deck and whether they're ready
  • The host fills, closes or opens seats and starts the game
  • Join a table from the list, or sit down at a season match

Zoom view

Zoom when it matters

When attackers, blockers or targets are chosen, the table zooms in on the creatures involved, with arrows from each attacker to what it attacks. Then it zooms back to the whole table.

  • Every battlefield on the table the rest of the time
  • Or a split layout with the side players in panels: your choice in the settings

Cards that come alive

Entrances and big moments

More than two thousand commanders step out of their cards as a cut-out figure in smoke and light, and hundreds of cards have their own hand-made effect.

  • Commander entrances, painted after each card's art
  • Blasphemous Act burns a pentagram over the table; Demonic Tutor opens a dark circle
  • Drains stream across the table, one line for each point of life
  • Natural sounds for every mechanic, creature voices and emotes

Board styles

Five tables to play on

Each style changes the whole look at once: the table, its pattern, the frames and the colors of every label. Here each one with a different game and other decks.

Draft and Sealed

Boosters like the real ones

Packs are put together the way Wizards packs them: the right slots and odds, special frames, bonus sheets and foils. Draft with seven bots that pick by real pick data, or open six boosters for Sealed.

  • Every recent set, cubes imported from CubeCobra, and Beejlander
  • Your rares and mythics revealed one by one, then 20 minutes to build
  • Three Swiss rounds, best of three, against the bots' decks

House rules

Your table, your rules

Pick fun house rules when you create a table. They're given to every player as emblems at the start of each game, so the rules engine plays them out for real.

Commanders cascade

Commander spells have cascade.

Monarch start

The last player starts as the monarch. Or the initiative, or a Treasure for everyone.

Planechase

Walk the planes, classic or extended.

Draw two each turn

Or a second wind: an empty hand draws at the end of your turn.

One of each name

A second copy of a permanent goes to the graveyard.

And more

Two land drops, haste, lifelink commanders, a tax cap of 6, only basic lands…

Seasons

A league for your group

Run a season over weeks: its rounds, bonus points for first blood or the best flavour, a table of standings, and each player's games and decks.

  • Matches played when the players are ready, or on a schedule
  • Bonus points for things like first blood or the best flavour
  • Commander drafts before a game: pick your legend from a few

Stipulations

A twist for every game

Give each game of a season its own rules for the decks: a budget, colors, a bracket, a creature type for the commander, no staples. Build them step by step or start from a template; the game checks every deck against them.

  • Budget from current card prices, power bracket, colors
  • Commander type, rarity and set; tribal and no-staples rules
  • Templates like Budget brew, Old legends or Tiny tribal

Profile

Make it yours

Your portrait at the table is any card's art; your card backs are your own sleeves. The Look tab sets the table's style, the font, the buttons and how commanders enter.

  • Avatar and sleeves from any card's art
  • Favorite lands for your basics
  • Your stats across all your games

Technical

The things that just work

Deck import and export

Bring decks in from a Moxfield or Archidekt link, a file or a pasted list (Arena exports too), and update them from their link later. Export a list with or without printings; import your collection from a CSV.

Replays

Every game is recorded. Watch any turn again from your seat, star the great games and keep them.

Rejoins

Lost the connection or closed the game? Start it again and the server hands your game back where it was. Resync table repairs a view that got out of step.

Art selection

Choose the printing and art of every card in your decks, your avatar from any card's art, and your sleeves.

Offline card search

Every card and printing with current prices, searchable in the deck builder without waiting for a server.

Updates itself

A new version shows in the main menu; one click downloads only what changed and restarts the game.

Free for your group, for Windows 10 and 11.
